How do I keep a chat scrolled to the newest message?
Assistant
Keep the log pinned to the end while the reader is there, and let go as soon as they scroll up. ChatLog measures instead of listening for scroll events, so this works on the native renderer too. Screen readers hear each reply once, when it is complete, not every fragment as it streams in.
