### Added

- **ui:** `NativeSelect`, `NativeSelectOption` and `NativeSelectGroup`: the browser's own select, styled like `Input` (experimental).
- **ui:** `ResizablePanelGroup`, `ResizablePanel` and `ResizableHandle`: panels with dividers you drag or move with the arrow keys, following the WAI-ARIA window splitter pattern; `resize_pair` is the pure resize rule (experimental).
- **e2e:** `tests/e2e/resizable.mjs` drives the resizable demo with the keyboard and the mouse.
