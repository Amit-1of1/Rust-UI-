### Added

- **cli:** `rust-ui theme --from-image <logo>` reads a PNG, JPEG or SVG logo, leaves out its backdrop, picks the most visible saturated colour as the brand colour and prints a contrast-fitted accent preset for it, with a comment listing the colours found and, when the logo has a clearly different second hue, an alternative `<name>-2` preset. Experimental. (docs/THEMING.md) (#61)
